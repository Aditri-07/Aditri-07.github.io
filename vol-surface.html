For one maturity at a time, the model's parameters are tuned until its curve sits as close as possible to that maturity's 9 implied vols. Press play to watch all six maturities get fitted in turn.

After Step A you have six separate curves. Step B never looks at the model again. It reads one number off each curve, at the same log-moneyness, and draws straight lines between them in total-variance space.
